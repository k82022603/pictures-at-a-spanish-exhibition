1970s symphonic progressive rock, analog and warm, tape saturation, wide dynamic range. Rickenbacker bass with a pick, front and centre. Grand piano, Hammond through a Leslie, strings, nylon-string guitar, quiet drums. 5/4 and 6/4. B-flat major, Phrygian colour.

Four sections:

First, solo grand piano states the promenade theme at quarter = 100, unhurried, one chord per four seconds. Bass is an independent countermelody in steady eighths, never root notes. A male voice enters plainly, almost spoken.

Second, tempo lifts to quarter = 132 then 144. Bright functional harmony, the most conventional writing here. Piano and bass lead, chords every 1.7 seconds. Lean and solitary. No vocals.

Third, fifteen seconds at quarter = 104. Nylon-string guitar takes the theme in canon, a second guitar one bar later. One short vocal line.

Fourth, a slow chorale at quarter = 72, church-like and hushed. Hammond and piano lead, strings behind. A descending figure G-F-Eb-D recurs. No drums, no vocals.
